Risk Disclosure

Last updated: November 2026

Trading and investing involve substantial risk of loss. FourCondition signals are technical screens provided for information only. They are not financial, investment or trading advice.

1. What a signal is, and isn't

A FourCondition signal means that, at the time of a scan, a ticker met a defined set of technical conditions (trend, momentum, volume and volatility) and cleared a set of risk filters at your strategy's thresholds. It does not consider your financial situation, objectives, tax position or risk tolerance, and it is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset.

2. No guarantee of results

Signals can and do fail. A setup that meets every condition can continue to fall. Filters reduce some known risks, such as earnings events or sharp declines, but cannot remove them. FourCondition does not guarantee any profit, return or level of accuracy.

3. Past performance

Historical performance, including the monthly reports published on this site, does not guarantee future results. Reports measure the price move after an alert. They are not the results of any trading account and do not include fees, commissions, spreads, slippage, taxes, or the timing of your own entries and exits.

4. Data and timing

Market data comes from third-party providers (including Yahoo Finance and Polygon.io) and may be delayed, incomplete or incorrect. Scans run at intervals (every 30 minutes), so prices can move between a scan, the delivery of a Telegram message and the moment you read it. Service interruptions on our side, at a data provider or at Telegram can cause signals to be late or missed.

5. Crypto and international markets

Cryptoassets are highly volatile, trade around the clock, are largely unregulated in many jurisdictions and can lose most or all of their value quickly. International listings carry currency, liquidity and market-hours risks.

6. Your responsibility

You alone decide whether and how to act on any signal. Only risk money you can afford to lose, do your own research, and consider speaking to a licensed financial adviser. Use of the Service is also governed by our Terms of Service.
