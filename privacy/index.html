Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Your Telegram user ID and username when you interact with the bot
  • Your watchlist and strategy preferences
  • Your subscription tier and billing status (handled by Paddle)
  • Quiz responses used to assign your investment strategy

2. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect payment card details — all payments are handled by Paddle
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties
  • We do not track your browsing or trading activity outside the bot

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To send you stock signals and alerts via Telegram
  • To personalise signals based on your strategy and watchlist
  • To manage your subscription and tier access

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on a private server hosted on Railway. Data is not shared with any third party except Paddle (for payment processing) and Telegram (for message delivery).

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you use the Service. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

  • You can request a copy of your data at any time
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time through Telegram

7. Contact

For any privacy concerns, email fourconditionbot@gmail.com.
