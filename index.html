How it works

Monitor. Evaluate. Notify.

Three steps, run automatically on every scan. You only see the last one.

  1. 01 — MONITOR

    Your watchlist, every 30 minutes

    FourCondition pulls market data for every ticker on your list: US stocks during market hours, crypto around the clock on Pro.

  2. 02 — EVALUATE

    Four conditions, eight filters

    Each ticker is checked against the four conditions at your strategy's thresholds, then against the risk filters.

  3. 03 — NOTIFY

    A message when all of it passes

    You get one Telegram message with the numbers behind it. A 5-day cooldown and a daily cap stop repeats.

The risk filters after the four conditions

A setup that passes all four conditions is still dropped if any of these trip.

Earnings blackout
No alerts from 5 days before to 3 days after an earnings report.
52-week low guard
Blocks anything within 8% of a 52-week low or a recent 10-session low.
Trend gate
Skips stocks more than 8% below their 200-day SMA, a sign of a real downtrend.
EPS trend
Requires positive forward earnings growth, with a whitelist for high-growth names. Crypto is exempt.
Velocity guard
Blocks falling knives: more than 12% under the 20-day MA, or a 5-session drop over 15%.
Penny stock filter
Ignores US stocks trading under $1.00.
5-day cooldown
One alert per ticker per setup, unless the 20-day MA moves more than 10%.
Daily cap
At most 3 alerts per ticker per day, so fast movers don't flood your chat.

About

A focused tool for structured market monitoring.

  • Rules over opinions: every signal can be traced to its numbers.
  • Quiet by design: fewer, better-qualified alerts.
  • Honest reporting: results published from real alerts.

FourCondition is a market-monitoring system delivered through Telegram. It watches a list of stocks and crypto, evaluates each one against four technical conditions and a set of risk filters, and speaks up only when everything lines up.

It started as a personal tool to take emotion out of research. Scrolling charts for hours produces a lot of opinions and very little structure, so the scanning, the checking and the filtering became code.

It is independent: no team, no investors. The screener runs live, and every filter exists because a tool that ignores earnings risk or collapsing stocks isn't worth using.

What is FourCondition?

A market-monitoring service that runs as a Telegram bot, @FourConditionBot. You give it a watchlist of stocks or crypto; it scans them every 30 minutes and messages you when a ticker meets all four of its technical conditions and clears its risk filters.

How does the four-condition system work?

Each ticker is checked for trend (price below its 20-day moving average), momentum (RSI(14) under your strategy's threshold), volume (on-balance volume higher than five sessions ago) and volatility (price within 2% of the lower Bollinger Band). All four must be true at once. Then eight risk filters, such as the earnings blackout and the velocity guard, must also pass.

What markets and assets are supported?

Any US-listed stock on every plan. Pro and Elite add crypto (BTC-USD, ETH-USD, SOL-USD and more), London (.L) and Asia (.HK, .T) listings. Market data comes from Yahoo Finance and Polygon.io.

How are signals generated?

Automatically, by rules. Every 30 minutes (US market hours for stocks, 24/7 for crypto) the bot pulls recent price history, calculates the indicators and runs the four conditions and filters with your strategy's thresholds. No one picks signals by hand.

Where do I receive notifications?

In your Telegram chat with @FourConditionBot. There is no separate app or account; everything, from your watchlist to settings and history, is managed with bot commands.

Is FourCondition financial advice?

No. FourCondition is a technical screening tool. A signal means certain conditions lined up on a chart. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell, and it does not know your circumstances. Always do your own research.

Does FourCondition guarantee results?

No. Signals can and do fail, and past results do not guarantee future ones. The filters reduce some known risks but cannot remove them. See the risk disclosure and the monthly reports above.

How can I get started?

Open @FourConditionBot in Telegram and press Start. Answer the 10-question quiz to pick a strategy, add tickers with /add or load a sector pack, and you're done. Free includes live alerts, a 150-ticker watchlist and all 10 strategies; Pro and Elite add more markets and history.

Why haven't I had a signal in a while?

All four conditions rarely line up at once, especially in strong trends. A quiet bot is working as intended. /topsignal shows which names are closest to passing.

What data do you store?

Your Telegram chat ID, watchlist, strategy settings and signal history. No name, email or card details. Data lives on Railway servers in the US. Email fourconditionbot@gmail.com to have it deleted within 7 days.
