About

Built by a 15-year-old
trader in Istanbul.

Independent No ads No financial advice

FourConditionBot started as a personal tool to remove emotion from trading decisions. The idea was simple: instead of manually checking dozens of indicators every day and making impulsive decisions, let a bot do the watching and only alert when everything lines up.

It's built and maintained by one person. No team, no investors, no agenda. Just a tool that does one thing well — signals when four independent conditions align across trend, momentum, volume, and volatility.

When all four conditions align simultaneously — RSI below threshold, price below moving average, OBV trending up, and price near the lower Bollinger Band — it sends you a Telegram alert. Additional filters suppress alerts near earnings dates, 52-week lows, and poor reward-to-risk setups.
