Signal logic

Four conditions. All must pass.

Every signal requires price, momentum, and volatility to align simultaneously. No single indicator is ever enough.

📉
Price below 20-day MA
The stock is trading below its short-term average — a potential pullback from a trend.
⚡
RSI below 50
Momentum is weakened but not yet in freefall. RSI below 50 signals cooling without capitulation.
📊
Near lower Bollinger Band
Price is within 2% of the lower band — statistically stretched to the downside.
🛡️
Passes all active filters
Earnings blackout, trend gate, 52-week low guard, EPS trend, and penny stock filter must all clear.
Smart filters

Signals that would likely fail are suppressed before they reach you.

📅 Earnings blackout Suppresses alerts 5 days before and 3 days after earnings reports
📉 52-week low guard Blocks signals within 5% of a 52-week low or recent 10-session lows
📏 Trend gate Skips stocks more than 8% below their 200-day SMA — in a true downtrend
💹 EPS trend filter Requires positive forward earnings growth (with whitelist exceptions for high-growth names)
🚫 Penny stock filter Removes any stock trading below $1.00 per share

Under the hood

What actually happens when a signal fires

Here's a real walkthrough of how the bot evaluates a stock, condition by condition.

Signal evaluation · Example
AAPL  ● PASS
$171.48
1
Price below 20-day Moving Average
✓ Pass
The bot fetches 20 days of closing prices and calculates the average. 20MA = $174.32. Current price $171.48 is below it — the stock has pulled back from its short-term trend. ✓
2
RSI (14) below 50
✓ Pass
RSI measures momentum over the last 14 candles. RSI = 43.7 — sellers have had more control than buyers recently, but it's not a full crash. Below 50 means momentum is weakened without being extreme. ✓
3
Near lower Bollinger Band
✓ Pass
Bollinger Bands are calculated using the 20MA ± 2 standard deviations. Lower band = $169.20. Price at $171.48 is within 1.3% of it — statistically stretched to the downside, meaning the move may be overdone. ✓
4
All smart filters pass
✓ Pass
Earnings are 34 days away (outside the 5-day blackout). Price is 4.2% above its 52-week low. The stock is only 2.1% below its 200SMA — well within the 8% trend gate. EPS growth is positive. Price is above $1. All filters clear. ✓
Signal fires 🚀
