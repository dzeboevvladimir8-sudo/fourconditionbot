Signal logic

Four conditions. All of them, at the same time.

No single indicator is ever enough. A message only goes out when every check below passes together.

01

Below the 20-day average

Price has pulled back from its short-term trend.

02

RSI under 50

Momentum has cooled, without the capitulation of a crash.

03

Near the lower band

Within 2% of the lower Bollinger Band, so the move is statistically stretched.

04

Every filter clears

Earnings, trend, 52-week low, velocity, EPS and penny-stock checks all pass.

The filters behind condition four

Setups that tend to fail are dropped before they reach you.

Earnings blackout
No alerts from 5 days before to 3 days after an earnings report.
52-week low guard
Blocks anything within 8% of a 52-week low or a recent 10-session low.
Trend gate
Skips stocks more than 8% below their 200-day SMA, which means they're in a real downtrend.
EPS trend
Requires positive forward earnings growth, with a whitelist for high-growth names. Crypto is exempt.
Velocity guard
Blocks falling knives: more than 12% under the 20-day MA, or a 5-session drop over 15%.
Penny stock filter
Ignores anything trading under $1.00.
5-day cooldown
One alert per ticker per setup, unless the 20-day MA moves more than 10%.
Daily cap
At most 3 alerts per ticker per day, so fast movers don't flood your chat.

Questions

Before you start.

Is this financial advice?

No. FourConditionBot is a technical screening tool. An alert means certain conditions lined up on a chart. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell. Always do your own research.

How do alerts work?

Every 30 minutes the bot scans your watchlist: US stocks during market hours, crypto 24/7. If an asset passes all four conditions and clears every filter, you get a Telegram message. A 5-day cooldown and a cap of 3 alerts per ticker per day stop repeats. Your strategy decides the exact thresholds.

What is the quiz for?

On your first message, 10 questions place you in one of ten strategies, from Conservative Income to fully Custom. The strategy sets your RSI level, band proximity and trend gate so alerts match how you actually invest. Retake it any time.

Is it free?

Yes. Free includes live alerts, a 150-ticker watchlist and all 10 strategies. Pro ($5/mo) adds crypto, London and Asia markets, signal history and the weekly digest. Elite ($10/mo) raises the watchlist to 999. Compare plans.

Which markets are covered?

Any US-listed stock, crypto (BTC-USD, ETH-USD, SOL-USD and more), London (.L) and Asia (.HK, .T) listings. Sector packs are free; crypto, London and Asia packs need Pro. Data comes from Yahoo Finance and Polygon.io.

What data do you store?

Your Telegram chat ID, watchlist, strategy settings and signal history. No name, email or card details. Data lives on Railway servers in the US. Email fourconditionbot@gmail.com to have it deleted within 7 days.

Why haven't I had an alert in a while?

All four conditions rarely line up at once, especially in strong trends. A quiet bot is working as intended.
